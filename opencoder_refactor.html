About Me

Hi, I'm Eric Van Camp. Welcome to my personal website! Feel free to reach out to me via any of the links in the footer of this page or on the contact page. I am a 6th year PhD candidate and NASA FINESST recipient in the department of Geophysical Sciences at the University of Chicago. Here, you can learn more about my research, projects, and get to know me better. My work is focused on modeling the chemical and dynamical evolution of protoplanetary disks. This includes studying how these processes affect the composition of the planets that form there. Check out this link for recent publications: Research . For more information, check out my CV or contact me at ericvc[at]uchicago[dot]edu.

I received my B.A. in Physics and Astronomy from Northwestern University and earned my Ph.D. from the University of Chicago. My undergraduate work focused on instrumentation and observation of star formation regions, as well as modeling the expected signal through the half-wave plate for the TolTEC polarimeter at the LMT.

Outside of work, I enjoy bouldering, hiking, and biking outside. When the weather is nice enough (which is not often in Chicago), I can run, swim, or bike to cities. When the weather isn't so nice, I like to stay at home for my dog, Gnome, and cat.

Interested in any of these hobbies? You can reach out to me on LinkedIn or Twitter.
